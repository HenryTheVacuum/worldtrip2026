WESTBOUND — offline dossier bundle
Toronto to Toronto, 15 Oct 2026 - 21 Feb 2027

Unzip anywhere and open index.html in any browser. No internet needed:
fonts are embedded, and the vaccination and flight-planner panels are built
into index.html itself. Every page links to its neighbours with plain relative
filenames, so the folder can be moved, copied to a phone, or put on a USB stick
as-is.

  index.html ....... the hub: route map, climate chart, clock, 27-stop
                     accordion, paperwork, flight planner
  <country>.html ... one dossier per stop, 27 of them
  fonts.css ........ the three typefaces, embedded (shared by every page)
